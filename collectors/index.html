Why Blue Dial Labs?

Search tools help you find watches. Blue Dial Labs helps you decide.

Marketplaces begin with what is for sale. Price tools begin with what a reference has been worth. Blue Dial Labs begins with the watch you want, the conditions under which you would actually buy it, and the evidence available across qualifying opportunities.

The goal is not to maximize transactions. It is to improve the collector's decision—even when the right answer is to wait or do nothing.

The Pursuit workflow

Define. Scout. Decide.

01 / Define

Start a Pursuit

Choose the reference and the constraints that matter: condition, completeness, geography, warranty, price thresholds, seller quality, and other supported preferences.

02 / Scout

Intelligence behind every Pursuit

Scout monitors supported supply, checks identity and offer context, normalizes comparable economics, and evaluates what it finds against the criteria in your Pursuit.

03 / Decide

Act only when the evidence supports it

A useful system should be able to say buy here, buy elsewhere, wait, or do nothing—without forcing the transaction into a proprietary marketplace.

Independent outcome

Collector alignment

Your thresholds are not partner inventory.

Buying criteria, collection information, rejection behavior, and other collector context are private by default. A partner relationship can make an offer available for evaluation; it does not entitle that partner to a collector's private intent without an explicit permission model.
