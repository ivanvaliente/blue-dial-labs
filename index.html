Collector-first by design

The platform works for the collector—not for a particular marketplace, retailer, dealer, advertiser, or affiliate partner.

What we are building

Intelligence across the watch lifecycle.

Watch-market evidence is scattered across different formats, currencies, conditions, geographies, and commercial channels. We are designing a durable evidence layer that keeps the reference—not a transient listing URL—at the center.

01 / Identity

Reference-centric market data

Organize observations around canonical watch references and meaningful variants, so evidence can accumulate coherently as individual offers appear and disappear.

03 / Continuity

From acquisition to ownership

Preserve useful continuity after a purchase, creating a foundation for ownership records and later sell-side decision support without becoming a marketplace.

Independent by construction

A decision system, not another sales channel.

Standard Time Labs is not a retailer, dealer, marketplace, affiliate-content site, or social network. The product is being designed to compare evidence and preserve the freedom to recommend the outcome that best fits the collector.

Operating principles

Trust is a product requirement.

Evidence with provenance

Source, observation time, and confidence should remain visible where they materially affect interpretation.

Confidence-aware matching

Low-confidence identity matches must not silently drive valuations, alerts, assignments, or recommendations.

Private by default

Collector intent and collection information warrant careful treatment because they may reveal sensitive preferences and significant wealth.

Commercially neutral

Compensation may support the business, but it cannot buy source inclusion, rank, confidence, or a recommendation.
