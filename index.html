Shared foundation

Identify. Contextualize. Decide.

01 / Identify

Know which watch the evidence is about.

Canonical identity, variants, aliases, provenance, and matching confidence reduce ambiguity before downstream decisions are made.

02 / Contextualize

Make market evidence comparable.

Offer data becomes more useful when identity, seller, condition, geography, transaction terms, and collector-specific economics are kept distinct and explicit.

03 / Decide

Optimize for the collector's supported outcome.

A product that works for the collector must be able to recommend acting, waiting, using another source, or doing nothing.

Trust across the portfolio

Current stage

Building the foundation before claiming the platform.

WristOwl is pre-launch

The acquisition-first collector workflow is being implemented and validated. Public collector-product access is not currently available.

WristAtlas is emerging infrastructure

The canonical capability is being built because WristOwl requires it. A public production API, coverage guarantees, pricing, and SLAs are not currently announced.

Names remain provisional

WristOwl and WristAtlas are working product candidates and remain subject to final legal and founder review.

Decision quality over feature count

The priority is proving that better identity, evidence, and context can improve a real collector decision before expanding the product surface.
