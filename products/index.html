Products

Identity underneath. Decision intelligence above it.

Blue Dial Labs is developing two complementary watch-software products. One establishes canonical watch identity. The other uses structured identity and market evidence to help collectors make better decisions.

The portfolio

Two products. Two distinct jobs.

The names WristOwl and WristAtlas remain working product candidates. The responsibility boundary is more important than the branding: WristAtlas identifies; WristOwl decides.

Collector decision intelligence

WristOwl

Define the watch and buying conditions that matter, compare qualifying opportunities across supported sources, understand collector-specific economics, and decide when an offer is worth pursuing—or when waiting is better.

Canonical watch identity

WristAtlas

A structured identity and reference-data layer for brands, collections, models, references, variants, aliases, provenance, and confidence-aware resolution.

Explore WristAtlas →

Shared thesis

Better watch decisions start with knowing exactly which watch the evidence is about.

How they fit

Separate responsibilities. Shared foundation.

01 / Identify

WristAtlas establishes identity.

Canonical references, variants, aliases, attributes, provenance, and confidence provide a stable vocabulary for watch software.

02 / Evaluate

WristOwl adds collector context.

Buying intent, qualifying market evidence, seller and transaction context, and Estimated Delivered Cost turn identity into a decision problem.

03 / Decide

The collector remains in control.

Commercial relationships cannot buy a better recommendation. Buy, wait, use another source, or do nothing must all remain possible outcomes.

In development

Neither product is being presented as a finished public platform.

WristOwl remains pre-launch. WristAtlas is being developed as the canonical identity product WristOwl depends on; public API access, coverage commitments, and commercial terms are not yet announced.
