WristOwl

Collector decision intelligence

Working product name · Pre-launch

WristOwl helps collectors decide when an opportunity is worth pursuing.

Instead of saving another listing URL, define the watch and buying conditions that matter. WristOwl is being built to monitor supported supply, normalize qualifying offers, add collector-specific economic context, and explain when the evidence supports action—or waiting.

Core operating principle

Track the watch and buying criteria, not a listing URL.

Product overview

Persistent buying intent becomes a decision system.

01 / Define

Create a Pursuit.

Start with a canonical watch reference and the purchase conditions that genuinely determine whether you would act.

02 / Compare

Evaluate qualifying opportunities.

Supported offers can be normalized for identity, condition, seller and transaction context, and bounded Estimated Delivered Cost.

03 / Decide

Get selective, explainable guidance.

A specific opportunity may be Worth Pursuing, Worth Considering, something to keep watching, or something that does not qualify.

Partner model

Useful supply without purchased preference.

Retailers, dealers, marketplaces, and data providers may contribute inventory or evidence. Commercial participation cannot buy a stronger collector recommendation.

Partner model →

Independent by construction

The recommendation belongs to the collector.

WristOwl must remain free to recommend a paying source, a non-paying source, waiting, transacting elsewhere, or doing nothing. Commercial relationships are separated from recommendation logic.
