Partnerships

Put the right watch offer in front of the right collector.

Traffic does not equal qualified demand.

Blue Dial Labs is designed to evaluate participating inventory against explicit collector purchase criteria—reference, condition, geography, warranty, price, and other relevant terms. When an offer genuinely fits, the collector can continue to the seller or marketplace's existing transaction surface with supported attribution preserved.

Why partners participate

Qualified relevance, not another impression.

The opportunity is to test whether a participating offer genuinely fits a collector-supplied decision—not to gain access to the collector's private buying policy.

01 / Intent

Explicit purchase criteria

A Pursuit can capture the target reference and the conditions that determine whether an offer is worth acting on. Those criteria remain private by default and are used by Blue Dial Labs to evaluate fit.

02 / Control

The existing transaction stays intact

Blue Dial Labs is designed as a discovery and decision layer, not a closed marketplace. The seller or marketplace keeps checkout, fulfillment, and the customer experience.

03 / Attribution

Attributable outbound traffic

Where the relationship supports it, links can preserve source and commercial attribution while sending the collector to the original transaction surface.

Commercial neutrality

Start small

A focused pilot does not require an enterprise integration.

A focused pilot can test whether participating inventory meaningfully matches collector-supplied purchase criteria, alongside identity quality, offer usefulness, and attribution.

01

Share a bounded data sample

Use an existing API, approved affiliate or product feed, scheduled structured export, or another agreed exchange covering a limited set of references or SKUs.

02

Normalize and qualify

Blue Dial Labs is designed to resolve product identity, seller, channel, all-in cost components, terms, source, and confidence before comparing qualifying offers against private collector criteria.

03

Measure useful behavior

Test whether the resulting offers produce relevant collector actions and attributable outbound traffic before considering broader coverage or deeper integration.

Responsible data access

Sanctioned data first.

The product is not designed around indiscriminate or unauthorized scraping. Production sources are evaluated for access rights, retention and redistribution terms, data quality, reliability, attribution, and operational risk.

Preferred

Partner-authorized interfaces

Official APIs, direct retailer or dealer feeds, and approved affiliate or product feeds provide the clearest operating path.

Licensed

Commercial data with defined rights

Licensed datasets can support identity, catalog, availability, or market evidence when permitted use and display boundaries are clear.

Exception

Narrow compliant monitoring

Site monitoring is considered only where legally, contractually, technically, and commercially appropriate—not as the foundation of the company.

Technical teams can review seller/channel/source distinctions, integration fields, and evidence conventions separately.
