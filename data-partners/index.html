Data & Partners

Qualified demand. Responsible data. Better commerce.

Standard Time Labs centers a collector decision layer on genuine purchase intent and the retailer, dealer, marketplace, or other seller whose offer best fits. We do not need to own the transaction to improve it.

Why work with us

Help the right collector find the right offer.

The goal is not to manufacture clicks or privilege a paying source. It is to turn fragmented market evidence and explicit collector criteria into higher-quality discovery. When a partner has the best-fitting offer, the collector should be able to reach that partner directly with attribution intact.

01 / Demand

Decision-stage collector intent

Acquisition Briefs capture a watch or reference plus meaningful purchase criteria such as condition, completeness, geography, warranty, and price thresholds. That creates a more useful decision context than a generic page view.

02 / Representation

Accurate, comparable offers

Structured product and offer data helps a partner's inventory be represented with the identity, availability, economics, warranty, and transaction terms that matter to a collector.

03 / Relationship

The seller keeps the transaction

Standard Time Labs is not a marketplace. A qualifying retailer, dealer, or marketplace remains the transaction endpoint and can retain its normal checkout, fulfillment, and customer relationship.

Partner types

Different partners, different value exchanges.

We do not treat every commercial relationship as the same integration. The useful question is what role the partner plays in helping a collector reach a better decision.

Commerce

Retailers, dealers & marketplaces

Partner value: qualified referrals, faithful offer representation, deep-link attribution, and a decision layer that can send the collector to the best-fitting seller without displacing the seller's transaction.

Technology

Affiliate & partnership platforms

Partner value: structured product feeds, approved commerce relationships, attribution infrastructure, and measurable referral outcomes while preserving separation between compensation and ranking.

Data

Catalog, distributor & data providers

Partner value: canonical product identity, authorized catalog or availability signals, accurate downstream representation, and clearly bounded use under agreed licensing and retention terms.

The commerce loop

From structured supply to attributable demand.

The model is intentionally simple: normalize authorized market evidence, compare it against explicit collector criteria, and route the collector to the best supported action. Commercial attribution is measured separately from recommendation logic.

  1. 01

    Partner feed or API

    Authorized product, offer, availability, or catalog data arrives through an agreed interface.

  2. 02

    Normalize and match

    Standard Time Labs resolves canonical identity, seller, channel, economics, terms, source, and confidence.

  3. 03

    Compare with collector intent

    A normalized offer is evaluated against an Acquisition Brief rather than shown merely because it exists.

  4. 04

    Surface a qualifying offer

    When the evidence supports action, the collector sees the relevant offer and the context needed to understand it.

  5. 05

    Click out with attribution

    The collector can continue to the seller or transaction venue while source and commercial attribution remain measurable.

Integration vocabulary

Seller is not channel is not source.

These roles may coincide, but they answer different questions. Preserving the distinction supports licensing, attribution, deduplication, seller evaluation, and meaningful comparison.

Who offers it?

Seller

The party that owns or offers the inventory: a dealer, retailer, brand, auction house, or private seller.

Where is it offered?

Sales channel

The venue where the collector can transact: a dealer website, marketplace, auction platform, showroom, or private market.

How did we learn?

Source

The route through which Standard Time Labs learned about the offer: an API, affiliate feed, direct partner feed, licensed provider, or other permitted input.

Example

A dealer may be the seller, a marketplace the sales channel, and the marketplace's approved API the source.

Useful integration fields

Context makes offers comparable.

No single source must provide every field. We value reliable, attributable signals that improve identity, availability, economics, and the collector's understanding of transaction terms.

Identity

  • Model and variant
  • Structured attributes
  • Source identifiers

Offer

  • Seller
  • Sales channel
  • Condition
  • Availability

Economics

  • Price and currency
  • Shipping
  • Geography
  • Known fees

Terms

  • Warranty
  • Returns
  • Fulfillment
  • Membership requirements

Evidence

  • Observation timestamp
  • Update timestamp
  • Provenance
  • Usage and retention rights

Partnership principles

Clear terms. Controlled use. Neutral outcomes.

01

Rights-aware by design

Access, retention, transformation, attribution, and display should reflect the applicable agreement and source policy.

02

Minimum necessary data

We prefer focused integrations that capture the fields needed for useful collector decisions without unnecessary data collection.

03

No purchased preference

A direct relationship or commercial payment can make data available; it cannot make an offer rank better or become the recommended action.
