Privacy

Private by default is a design constraint.

This notice describes the limited information handling associated with the Blue Dial Labs corporate website.

Last updated August 11, 2026

At a glance

  • No collector accounts
  • No collection-data intake
  • No advertising trackers
  • No behavioral profiling
  • Contact form composes email locally
privacy@bluediallabs.com ↗

01

Scope of this notice

This notice applies to the Blue Dial Labs corporate website at www.bluediallabs.com. The site provides company and product information. It does not provide collector accounts, accept watch-collection information, or operate the collector-facing platform.

Before collector accounts, acquisition criteria, collection information, or product-usage data are handled, a separate and more comprehensive collector-product privacy notice will apply.

02

Information you choose to send

The contact form on this site prepares an email in your configured mail application. The form does not submit the entered information to a Blue Dial Labs website database. Nothing is sent to Blue Dial Labs until you choose to send the email from your mail application.

If you email Blue Dial Labs, we receive the contact information and message content you provide. We may retain that information as reasonably necessary to respond, continue the conversation, maintain a business relationship, keep appropriate business records, and meet legal obligations.

Please do not send sensitive watch-collection, financial, credential, or identity information through general email unless it is necessary and an appropriate secure method has been agreed.

03

Website infrastructure data

Normal hosting, network, and security infrastructure may process technical request data such as IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, referring page, and security events. This information may be used to deliver the site, maintain reliability, investigate abuse, and protect the service.

Hosting providers may process this technical data under their own terms and privacy practices.

04

Tracking and advertising

As of the date above, this site does not deploy advertising pixels, third-party analytics tags, behavioral profiling, or marketing cookies. Because the current site does not use those technologies, it does not display a cookie-consent banner.

If the site's technology or information practices materially change, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated before or when that change takes effect.

05

Sharing and disclosure

Information may be processed by service providers that support email, hosting, security, or business operations, subject to their applicable terms. Blue Dial Labs may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and security, or address fraud or abuse.

We do not describe email contacts as a source of collector profiles, and we do not state that we sell website visitor information.

06

Retention and requests

Information is retained for the period reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including legitimate business-record and legal needs. Retention may vary with the nature of the relationship and applicable obligations.

For a privacy question or request concerning information you sent to Blue Dial Labs, contact privacy@bluediallabs.com. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

07

Collector-product privacy

A watch collection can reveal significant wealth, purchasing intent, location patterns, and personal preferences. Collector data, buying thresholds, constraints, and behavioral intent are therefore private by default in the product model. Any permissioned sharing features will require explicit product rules and a dedicated privacy framework before they are offered.
