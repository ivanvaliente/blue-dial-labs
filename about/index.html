About Standard Time Labs

Independent by design. Collector-aligned by construction.

Standard Time Labs is an independent software company developing decision and data infrastructure for wrist-watch collectors. The company and product are in active development.

Why we exist

Watch-market information is abundant. Decision-quality evidence is not.

Collectors routinely move between brands, retailers, dealers, marketplaces, auctions, and private markets. Offers are difficult to compare because identity, condition, geography, fees, warranty, seller quality, and transaction terms are represented differently across sources.

01 / Decision layer

Improve the decision without owning the transaction.

Standard Time Labs is being designed to help a collector compare evidence and choose the best available action. When a retailer, dealer, or marketplace has the best-fitting offer, the product should be able to send the collector there rather than force the transaction into a proprietary marketplace.

02 / Neutrality

Commercial relationships do not buy preference.

Compensation may support the business, but it cannot improve source inclusion, offer rank, stated confidence, or a collector-facing recommendation.

03 / Infrastructure

Canonical identity before superficial aggregation.

The technical foundation centers on reference-level identity, attributable observations, comparable offer economics, and confidence-aware matching.

Founder

Founded by Ivan Valiente.

Standard Time Labs was founded to build software that stays aligned with the collector across buying, ownership, and eventual selling decisions—rather than optimizing for transaction volume inside a single commercial channel.

Company stage

Early, explicit, and open to focused collaboration.

The product is in development. The company is validating data access, normalization, collector workflows, and commercial relationships deliberately. References to potential partner types describe the model being developed; they do not imply an existing relationship with any particular company.

How we intend to work

Small pilots. Clear terms. Measurable value.

01

Start narrow

Prefer a bounded set of references, SKUs, or offers over a large integration before either side has validated the value exchange.

02

Use authorized data paths

Prefer official APIs, affiliate or product feeds, direct partner feeds, and appropriately licensed commercial data.

03

Preserve attribution

Keep seller, channel, source, and commercial attribution distinct so traffic and outcomes can be measured without corrupting recommendation logic.

04

Protect collector intent

Buying thresholds, preferences, collection information, and behavioral intent are private by default and should not become a partner targeting feed without an explicit permission model.

Work with us

We welcome early conversations about a narrow pilot, structured product or offer data, attribution, and technical fit.
