About Blue Dial Labs

Independent by design. Collector-aligned by construction.

Blue Dial Labs is an independent software company focused on decision and data infrastructure for wrist-watch collectors.

Why we exist

Watch-market information is abundant. Decision-quality evidence is not.

Collectors move between brands, retailers, dealers, marketplaces, auctions, and private markets. Offers are difficult to compare because identity, condition, geography, fees, warranty, seller quality, and transaction terms are represented differently across sources.

01 / Principle

The collector is the client of the decision.

The platform is designed to optimize for the collector's supported outcome, not for transaction volume inside a particular marketplace or commercial channel.

02 / Independence

Commercial relationships do not buy preference.

Compensation may support the business, but it cannot improve source inclusion, offer rank, stated confidence, or a collector-facing recommendation.

03 / Infrastructure

Structure makes better decisions possible.

Reference-level identity, attributable evidence, comparable economics, and confidence-aware matching create the foundation for useful recommendations.

Founder & origin

Built by a collector with a systems background.

Ivan Valiente is the founder of Blue Dial Labs and a Systems Architect and Software Engineer with more than two decades of experience designing, building, and deploying scalable web applications, e-commerce systems, and enterprise backend platforms. His background spans software architecture, object-oriented design, and taking complex technical projects from concept through production. Blue Dial Labs grew from a simple principle: purchase decisions should be optimized for the collector, not for the economics of a particular marketplace or sales channel.

Where we are today

Blue Dial Labs is currently pre-launch.

We are developing and validating the initial collector acquisition workflow and exploring focused data and partner pilots. Current work centers on Pursuits, acquisition intelligence, canonical watch identity, comparable market evidence, privacy, and commercially neutral partner participation.
