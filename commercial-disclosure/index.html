The Blue Dial Labs Promise

Revenue can support the product. It cannot steer the answer.

Blue Dial Labs is built around a collector-first promise: commercial relationships may support the business, but they must not change which evidence is shown, how it is ranked, how confident the system claims to be, or what action is recommended.

The promise

The recommendation belongs to the collector—not to whoever pays Blue Dial Labs.

Three commitments

What the promise means in practice.

Commercial independence is not a branding claim. It is a set of operating constraints that must remain true as Blue Dial Labs develops revenue relationships.

01

The recommendation belongs to the collector

Compensation cannot buy source inclusion, a higher offer rank, stronger stated confidence, favorable economic treatment, or a collector-facing recommendation.

02

The best answer may produce no revenue

The system must remain free to recommend buying elsewhere, waiting, selling through another route, or doing nothing when that outcome better serves the collector.

03

Commercial relationships are disclosed

Affiliate, referral, licensing, and partnership relationships may support the business, but they are identified separately from the logic used to compare evidence and recommend an action.

The practical test

The best outcome may produce no revenue.

A collector-aligned system must remain willing to recommend the decision supported by the evidence and the collector's context—even when that decision does not benefit Blue Dial Labs commercially.

Commercial disclosure

Relationships that may support the business.

Commercial relationships may include several models. This page does not claim that any particular program, partnership, source access, or revenue stream currently exists.

01

Affiliate programs

The company may receive compensation when a collector follows a qualifying link and completes an eligible transaction.

02

Referral arrangements

A retailer, marketplace, dealer, or service provider may compensate the company for attributable introductions or transactions.

03

Data licensing

The company may license data from providers or, where rights permit, license selected data or analysis to customers or partners. A WristAtlas data relationship must not create hidden preference in WristOwl.

04

Commercial partnerships

The company may enter direct technical, data, marketing, or business relationships with industry participants.
