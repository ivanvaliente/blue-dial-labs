Commercial disclosure

Revenue can support the product. It cannot steer the answer.

Standard Time Labs is building an independent decision platform for collectors. The business model must preserve that relationship, including when a source or partner pays the company.

The rule

Commercial compensation does not determine whether a source is included, how an offer is ranked, or what action is recommended.

Potential relationships

Commercial activity, clearly disclosed.

Standard Time Labs is still in development. It may eventually use several commercial models, but this page does not claim that any particular program, partnership, source access, or revenue stream currently exists.

01

Affiliate programs

The company may receive compensation when a collector follows a qualifying link and completes an eligible transaction.

02

Referral arrangements

A retailer, marketplace, dealer, or service provider may compensate the company for attributable introductions or transactions.

03

Data licensing

The company may license data from providers or, where rights permit, license selected data or analysis to customers or partners.

04

Commercial partnerships

The company may enter direct technical, data, marketing, or business relationships with industry participants.

Commercial metadata may be used for attribution, disclosure, and revenue accounting—but must remain separate from collector-facing ranking and recommendation logic.

The practical test

The best outcome may produce no revenue.

A collector-aligned system must remain willing to recommend the decision supported by the evidence and the collector's context—even when that decision does not benefit Standard Time Labs commercially.
