# Blue Dial Labs Visual Precision Refinement v0.1

## Status

Implemented as a focused website refinement on top of Blue Dial Labs Visual Identity v1.2.

This is not a replacement brand system or a broad website redesign. The established typography stack, warm editorial paper surface, navy/blue identity, navigation, information architecture, and mobile behavior remain intact.

## Objective

Make Blue Dial Labs increasingly look like the instrument it claims to be:

**messy market information → structured evidence → better decision**

The site should combine watch-collector familiarity with software/data precision without drifting into luxury-retailer imagery, generic SaaS styling, or decorative data-tech motifs.

## Visual principle

**Evidence is the art.**

Prefer graphics that explain a real product responsibility over graphics that merely decorate the page.

Good visual subjects include:

- ambiguous source observations resolving toward canonical identity;
- Brand → Collection → Model → Reference → Variant;
- confidence-aware identity resolution;
- collector Pursuit criteria;
- offer normalization;
- delivered-price components;
- decision states and supported outcomes;
- provenance and evidence relationships.

## Implemented homepage treatment

### Reference Instrument

The former text-led portfolio card is replaced by an illustrative evidence flow:

1. synthetic ambiguous market text;
2. WristAtlas identity resolution through the canonical hierarchy;
3. WristOwl collector-context decision outcomes; and
4. the shared principle: structured evidence before recommendation.

The example is explicitly labeled synthetic and illustrative so it does not imply live product coverage or a production recommendation.

### Product responsibility signals

The two homepage product cards now show their jobs as compact diagrams:

- WristOwl: `Pursuit → Evidence → Decision`
- WristAtlas: `Brand → Collection → Model → Reference → Variant`

These diagrams supplement rather than replace explanatory copy.

### Abstract reference field

The dark corporate principle band receives restrained circular reference geometry. It evokes a measurement/reference instrument without depicting a literal wristwatch, movement, gear, crown, or luxury-product silhouette.

### Interaction polish

Buttons and text links receive short, restrained hover transitions. Motion is removed when the user requests reduced motion.

## Invariants retained

- Keep the warm paper background as the editorial/collector layer.
- Use crisp light surfaces for evidence and data objects.
- Keep navy as the strong structural/trust surface.
- Keep blue as the primary identity and interaction color.
- Keep copper red sparse and non-interactive.
- Retain serif + sans-serif + monospace typography roles.
- Remain private-by-default and trust-forward in messaging.
- Do not imply live coverage, integrations, partnerships, or production capabilities that are not announced.

## Explicitly avoided

Do not use the following as the primary visual system:

- hero photography of luxury watches;
- faded collages of recognizable timepieces;
- lifestyle wrist photography;
- gears, crowns, movements, or watch silhouettes as generic decoration;
- glowing network diagrams;
- fake trading terminals;
- AI-style gradients or glow effects;
- decorative animation that does not explain product behavior.

Actual watch imagery may be reconsidered later for specific editorial or product-detail use cases, but it should not make Blue Dial Labs resemble a retailer, marketplace, watch manufacturer, or magazine.

## Future extension

Apply the same visual grammar selectively to deeper pages where it improves comprehension:

- WristAtlas: raw input → candidate identity → canonical output → downstream value;
- WristOwl: Pursuit → normalized opportunities → policy comparison → decision change;
- Trust: provenance, confidence, and commercial-independence diagrams;
- Data Standards: canonical hierarchy and evidence lineage.

Do not add diagrams simply to fill visual space. Every graphic should reduce explanation burden, reinforce a real system concept, or make a trust boundary easier to understand.
